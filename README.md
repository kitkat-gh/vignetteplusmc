# Vignette ➕
Adds customizable _vignettes_ ( borders around your screen ) to Minecraft when certain things happen\
\
**There are currently 3 vignettes added into the mod** 🟢
- Low Health 💔
- Hunger 🍴
- Currently Drowning 🫧

_**You can suggest more in the [discord linked on the side!](https://discord.gg/TkwWKbY2tC)**_
## Highly configurable ⚙️
![The Hunger Vignette tab of the mod settings](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/7f99fd3bf830c7635be18888f7a2296d1ce71eee.png)
You can make your experience with this mod exactly how you want, with the **20** current settings you can tweak, and adjust!\
\
You can adjust the color, the thresholds the vignettes appear at, and you can even remove one of the vignettes if you think it's not for you, or too annoying

## Keybindings ⌨️
_Need a quicker solution to opening the config menu?_\
Just press the **V key** to open the Config menu when your in a pinch, or whatever you set your Config Menu keybind to!\
\
You can also toggle on and off the mod by pressing **F12**, if you don't want to go all the way to go the Config Menu and change the Mod Enabled setting!
